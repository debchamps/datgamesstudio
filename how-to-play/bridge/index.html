How to play Bridge

Contract bridge is the deepest of the trick-taking games, and easier to start than its reputation. Count your points, win the auction, then play your hand and your partner's.

  • 4 players in 2 teams
  • 52-card deck
  • Auction, then play
  • Chicago scoring

Rules at a glance

  1. Count points: A 4, K 3, Q 2, J 1. About 12 opens.
  2. A bid = tricks over six + a trump suit or No Trump.
  3. Each bid beats the last: ♣ ♦ ♥ ♠ NT, low to high.
  4. Declarer plays both their own hand and dummy's.
  5. Make the contract to score; bonuses for game and slam.

Setup

Deal 13 cards each. You (South) and North are partners against East and West. Count your high-card points: Ace 4, King 3, Queen 2, Jack 1. The pack holds 40; about 12 is enough to open the bidding.

The auction

Starting with the dealer, each player bids, passes or doubles. A bid is a number of tricks over six plus a trump suit or No Trump: 2♥ means 8 tricks with hearts as trumps.

Each bid must beat the last: a higher number, or the same number in a higher strain (♣ ♦ ♥ ♠ NT, low to high). Three passes in a row end the auction, and the last bid is the contract. If all four pass, the cards are redealt.

Declarer and dummy

The declarer is the partner who first named the contract's strain, not necessarily the last to bid. The player on declarer's left leads, then the dummy (declarer's partner) lays their hand face up, and declarer plays both hands.

The play

Follow the suit led if you can, or play any card. The highest trump wins, or else the highest card of the suit led. In No Trump there are no trumps.

Scoring

  • Make the contract and your side scores per trick bid: 20 in ♣ ♦, 30 in ♥ ♠, and in No Trump 40 for the first trick then 30.
  • Reaching 100 in one contract (3NT, 4♥, 4♠, 5♣, 5♦) earns a game bonus. 12 or 13 tricks bid and made are slams.
  • Fall short and the defenders score for each trick you're down. Doubles and vulnerability raise the stakes.

Chicago

The app scores bridge as Chicago: four deals with nobody vulnerable, then the dealer's side twice, then both. Most points wins.

Tips to win

  • Turn on guided bidding in Settings: the app highlights the suggested call, and you can press and hold any call to see what it means.
  • As declarer, count your sure winners before playing to trick one.
  • With trumps, draw the opponents' trumps early unless you need them to ruff in dummy.

FAQ

Do I need a partner to play bridge?

Not in our app. You play against three computer opponents, and you play your partner's hand whenever your side declares.

Which bidding system does the app use?

Standard American (SAYC) for all four players.
