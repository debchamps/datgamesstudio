How to play Hearts

Hearts is the classic game of avoiding points. Every heart you take costs you, the Queen of Spades costs a lot more, and the lowest score wins, unless someone shoots the moon.

Rules at a glance

  1. Pass 3 cards before each deal (left, right, across, hold).

  3. Each heart = 1 point. Q♠ = 13.
  4. No leading hearts until one has been played.

Setup and passing

Deal 13 cards to each player. Before play, everyone passes 3 cards: to the left on the first deal, then right, then across, then no pass, and repeat.

Playing a trick

Whoever holds the 2♣ leads it to the first trick. Follow the suit led if you can; if not, play any card. There is no trump: the highest card of the led suit wins.

You can't lead hearts until a heart has been played on another suit (“hearts are broken”).

Scoring

Each heart you capture is 1 point and the Q♠ is 13, so a deal holds 26 points. Points are bad.

Shoot the moon: capture all 26 and you score 0 while everyone else gets 26.

Winning

The match ends when someone reaches 100. The player with the lowest score wins.

Tips to win

  • Pass away high spades (A♠, K♠) if you don't hold the Q♠; they can catch it.
  • Try to get void in a suit early so you can dump hearts and the Q♠ on others.
  • Watch for a moon shot: if one player is taking every heart, keep one heart for yourself.

FAQ

What is shooting the moon?

Taking all 13 hearts and the Q♠ in one deal. Instead of 26 points, you score 0 and every opponent gets 26.

How is Bray different from Hearts?

Bray has no passing, no 2♣ rule, hearts can be led any time, the Q♠ is worth 12 and taking no tricks earns a −12 bonus.
