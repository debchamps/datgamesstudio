How to play Hokm

Hokm (حکم) is the classic Persian partnership game. There is no auction: the Hâkem picks trump after seeing just five cards, and the first team to seven tricks wins the hand.

  • 4 players in 2 teams
  • 52-card deck
  • First to 7 tricks
  • Game to 7 points

Rules at a glance

  1. The Hâkem sees 5 cards and names trump.
  2. Follow the suit led if you can.
  3. First team to 7 tricks wins the hand.
  4. A sweep (Kot) scores extra.
  5. First team to 7 points wins.

Setup and the Hâkem

Partners sit opposite. One player is the Hâkem (ruler). The Hâkem is dealt 5 cards first and declares the trump suit from those five. Then the rest of the deck is dealt so everyone holds 13.

Playing a trick

The Hâkem leads the first trick. Follow the suit led if you can; otherwise play any card. The highest trump wins, or the highest card of the suit led if no trump was played.

Winning a hand

The first team to take 7 tricks wins the hand and scores 1 point. If the hand is a Kot (a 7–0 sweep), it is worth more: 2 points when the Hâkem's team sweeps, 3 when the opponents sweep the Hâkem.

Winning the game

The first team to 7 points wins. If the Hâkem's team loses a hand, the Hâkem role passes to the next player.

Court Piece

The app also includes Court Piece, the South Asian version of the same game, where a sweep (a “court”) is worth 2 to either side.

Tips to win

  • As Hâkem, pick the suit where your five cards are longest, not just highest.
  • Count trumps: there are 13, and knowing how many are left wins the late tricks.
  • Watch for a Kot: if the opponents have none after five tricks, fight hard for just one.

FAQ

What does Hokm mean?

It means “command” in Persian: the Hâkem commands the trump suit.
