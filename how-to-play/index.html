Clear, short rules for every DAT Games title, written to match exactly how each game plays in our apps. Pick a game, learn it in a few minutes, then deal yourself in.

Callbreak

Spades are always trump. Before play, call 1 to 8 tricks.

Bid 7 to 13 tricks for your team. The highest bidder names trump.

Bridge
