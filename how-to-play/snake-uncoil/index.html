How to play Snake Uncoil

Snake Uncoil is a calm logic puzzle with one question: which snake can move next? Tap a snake and it slides straight out the way its head faces. Free them all to clear the board.

  • 600 levels
  • 12 worlds
  • Daily challenge
  • No timers

Rules at a glance

  1. Tap a snake to slide it out the way it faces.
  2. It only escapes if nothing blocks its path.
  3. A blocked snake bumps back and costs a heart.
  4. You have three hearts per level.
  5. Clear every snake to win.

The goal

Each level is a board of tangled snakes. Clear every snake off the board to win.

Moving snakes

Tap a snake and it slides straight out in the direction its head faces. It can only escape if nothing blocks its path to the edge. Tap a blocked snake and it bumps back, costing one of your three hearts.

Stars and hints

Clear a board in fewer taps for three stars. Stuck? A hint shows you a snake that can move. There are no timers, so think as long as you like.

Twists

As you progress, new mechanics appear: sleepy snakes, locked gates and golden key snakes, tall grass, tunnels, frozen and vine-tied snakes, ghost snakes, crystal mirrors that bend the path, and more.

Daily challenge

A fresh puzzle every day, from an easy Monday to an expert Sunday. Keep your streak going.

Tips to win

  • Start from the edges: snakes pointing outward near the border are usually free.
  • Before tapping, trace the line from the head to the edge.
  • On big boards, pinch or tap Zoom to check for hidden blockers.

FAQ

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every board has a solution.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Gameplay works without a connection.
