How this Bray game works

  • Four players, 13 cards each, every player on their own. There is no passing and no trump suit.
  • The lead rotates each deal, and any card may be led, hearts included. Follow suit if you can. The highest card of the suit led wins.
  • Each heart is 1 point and the Q♠ is 12, so a deal holds 25 penalty points.
  • Take no tricks at all in a deal and you score −12.
  • The game ends when someone reaches 100. The lowest score wins.
