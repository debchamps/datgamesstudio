How this Callbreak game works

  • Four players, 52 cards, 13 tricks per deal. A match is 5 deals.
  • Before play, call 1 to 8 tricks. Make your call to score it; every extra trick adds 0.1.
  • Follow suit and beat the winning card whenever you can. Out of the suit? You must play a spade.
  • Miss your call and you lose it. Highest total after 5 deals wins.
