How this Hearts game works

  • Four players, 13 cards each. Before play, pass 3 cards: left, right, across, then hold (no pass), and repeat.
  • Whoever holds the 2♣ leads it. Follow suit if you can. There is no trump, so the highest card of the suit led wins the trick.
  • Each heart is 1 point and the Q♠ is 13. You can't lead a heart until hearts are broken.
  • Shoot the moon by taking every penalty card: you score 0 and everyone else gets 26.
  • The game ends when someone reaches 100. The lowest score wins.

This browser version uses the standard rules. The app adds achievements, stats, themes and optional rule variations.
