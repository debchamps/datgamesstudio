How to play Snake Uncoil

  • Tap a snake and it slides out the way its eyes look, all the way off the board.
  • It only goes if nothing is in its way. A blocked snake bumps and comes back.
  • Free every snake to clear the level. Fewer bumps earn more stars.
  • Every fifth level hides a picture under the snakes. Clear it to see it.
  • Stuck? 💡 Hint shows a snake that can move. Each new level you clear earns one.
