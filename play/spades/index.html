How this Spades game works

  • You and Arthur are partners against Emma and William. All 52 cards are dealt, 13 each.
  • Each player bids the tricks they expect to take. Partners' bids add up to the team contract. Bid Nil to promise no tricks at all.
  • Spades are always trump, and can't be led until one has been played. Follow suit if you can.
  • Make your contract: 10 per trick bid plus 1 per extra trick. Miss it: lose 10 per trick bid. A Nil is worth ±100.
  • First team to 500 wins. A team that falls to −200 loses (mercy rule).

This browser version plays standard partnership Spades. The app adds Cutthroat, Jokers, Blind Nil, the Board rule and sandbag penalties.
