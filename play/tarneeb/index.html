How this Tarneeb game works

  • You and Arthur are partners against Emma and William. All 52 cards are dealt, 13 each.
  • Bid 7 to 13 tricks your team will take, or pass. The highest bidder names the trump suit.
  • Follow suit if you can. The highest trump wins a trick, otherwise the highest card of the suit led.
  • Make your bid and score every trick you took. Miss it and you lose the bid while the defenders score their tricks.
  • The first team to 41 wins the match. If everyone passes, the hand is redealt.

This browser version plays Standard Tarneeb. The app also has Egyptian (double/redouble) and Syrian 41 variants, English and Arabic, and more.
