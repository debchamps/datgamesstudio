Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy applies to DAT Games Studio mobile games and this website. It explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and which third-party services may process information when you use the games.

Information We May Collect

DAT Games Studio games may collect or receive limited information needed to operate, improve, secure, and monetize the games. This can include:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage information, such as game events, session activity, achievements, preferences, and performance signals.
  • Advertising identifiers or similar identifiers used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and frequency controls.
  • Optional information you choose to provide, such as feedback submitted through store reviews, social pages, or direct contact.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to run the games, save preferences, improve gameplay, analyze crashes, understand player engagement, show ads where applicable, prevent abuse, and respond to feedback.

Third-Party Services

The games may use third-party services that collect information according to their own policies. These services can include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)
  • Meta / Facebook services
  • AppLovin
  • Tappx

Availability of these services can vary by game, platform, country, and app version.

Advertising And Your Choices

Some games are supported by advertising. Where ads are shown, an advertising identifier may be used to deliver and measure them, to limit how often the same ad is repeated, and to detect fraud. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your region and your choices.

  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. Games that show ads present a consent choice, provided through Google's User Messaging Platform, before personalised advertising is used. You may accept or refuse personalised advertising. Refusing does not stop you playing; you will simply see non-personalised ads. In games that use Firebase Analytics, the same choice also controls whether analytics data may be stored and used for advertising, and until you have made a choice it is not. Where a game offers it, you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen.
  • United States. Residents of certain states are shown a privacy notice in games that serve ads, and may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through that notice.
  • Any device. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time — on Android under Settings › Google › Ads, and on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Deleting it prevents apps from using it to personalise advertising.

Data Retention

The games do not require an account, so no name, email address or profile is collected. Game progress, statistics and preferences are stored on your own device and are removed when you uninstall the game. Information processed by the third-party services listed above is kept as follows:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are kept for up to 90 days.
  • User-level analytics data (Firebase / Google Analytics) is kept for no longer than 14 months; only aggregated, non-identifying reports are kept beyond that.
  • Advertising data processed by Google AdMob and other ad networks is retained under those providers' own policies, linked above.

Your Rights And Deletion Requests

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information relating to you, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, email majumdar.debarghya@gmail.com with the name of the game. Because the games have no accounts, we may ask for details that help identify your installation. We aim to respond within 30 days. You can also remove all game data stored on your device by uninstalling the game, and reset your advertising identifier as described above.

Cookies And Website Data

This website is hosted as a static GitHub Pages site. It does not run its own account system. External links, app stores, social pages, and embedded third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Children's Privacy

DAT Games Studio does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email majumdar.debarghya@gmail.com so the information can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed through the games and services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

External Links

The games and website may link to app stores, social networks, support pages, ads, or other third-party websites. DAT Games Studio is not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of those third-party services.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email majumdar.debarghya@gmail.com. You can also reach us through the relevant app store listing or the DAT Games Facebook page.
